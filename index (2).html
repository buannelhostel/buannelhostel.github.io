Buannel Hall of Residence

Named after the birthplace of animals and the abode of fairies in Mizo mythology.

Buannel Hall of Residence is a temporary hostel for men research scholars, established in August 2017. It is located near the Basketball Court on the Mizoram University campus, and is equipped with a warden's quarter. Every student is provided with a study table, a chair, and a bed, and each room has an attached cupboard and book-shelf.

About the hall

Buannel takes its name from Mizo mythology, where it is described as the birthplace of animals and the abode of fairies. As one of the Mizoram University's twelve halls of residence, Buannel provides temporary accommodation for men pursuing research at the University, in a setting close to the Basketball Court on campus.

The hall is a small, closely-knit community by design — with 39 seats, it is one of the more compact halls of residence at MZU, and is overseen by a resident warden from the University faculty, supported by student office bearers elected or appointed from among the residents.
